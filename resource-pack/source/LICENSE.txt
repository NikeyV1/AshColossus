Ash Colossus resource pack 1.x (Wither Boss by NikeyV1 / Buffed SMP)

Origin
All models, textures, animations, sound effects and music of this pack were created from
scratch for this project using procedural generation and synthesis. The generation scripts
are not included in this repository.
No samples, third-party textures, third-party models or third-party music were used.
